# Arryn

Standards and interoperability.

> As High as Honor.

Motto: house words; see [provenance](../sources.md).

## When to use

Choosing or changing protocols, formats, public APIs, conventions or compatibility promises.

## Axioms

1. **Look for the established standard first.** Prefer recognized protocols, formats and conventions.
2. **Conformance is demonstrated.** Validate contracts and interoperability where they matter.
3. **Exceptions need reasons.** Record why a standard does not fit and what maintaining the alternative entails.
4. **Compatibility is an explicit promise.** Version and evolve public contracts deliberately.

## Application and evidence

Identify the applicable specification and version, then verify the affected contract. Reuse project standards. Record material deviations and compatibility tests; do not invent a new standard to solve a local naming preference.
