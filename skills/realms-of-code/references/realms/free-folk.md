# The Free Folk

Supply-chain engineering.

> Choose freely. Trust deliberately.

Motto: original engineering motto; see [provenance](../sources.md).

## When to use

Adding, updating, releasing or retiring dependencies and third-party artifacts.

## Axioms

1. **Know what enters the system.** Maintain an accurate inventory of dependencies and artifacts.
2. **Know where it came from.** Verify provenance, integrity and appropriate version constraints.
3. **Ownership continues after adoption.** Track vulnerabilities, upstream health, licenses and upgrades.
4. **Keep the supply chain lean.** Remove unused dependencies and unnecessary transitive exposure.

## Application and evidence

Use the project's manifests, lockfiles and existing supply-chain checks. Review changes to direct and transitive dependencies and verify relevant integrity and vulnerability results. Braavos decides whether to adopt; Free Folk maintains confidence in what is adopted.
