# Charter and motto provenance

The engineering assignments and axioms were developed with Elie Bursztein on
2026-10-18. They use fictional factions as mnemonic inspiration. They are
original engineering guidance; a faction's fictional conduct is not a software
standard. No scripts, artwork or project-specific rules from Capsem are copied.

## Literary inspirations

- [House words](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/House_words): Martell,
  Tyrell, Tully, Arryn, Baratheon and Stark use their established house words.
- [Daenerys in Hardhome](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3866850/characters/nm3592338/):
  the wheel quotation is character dialogue, not House Targaryen's house words.
- [Lannister and Iron Bank sayings](https://gameofthrones.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Bank_of_Braavos):
  the debt sayings are associated sayings, not official house words.
- [Drowned God](https://gameofthrones.fandom.com/wiki/Drowned_God): Greyjoy's
  selected line is an ironborn religious saying, not the house's official words.
- [Night's Watch](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Night%27s_Watch): the
  selected line is an excerpt from its oath.
- [Dragonstone dialogue](https://genius.com/Game-of-thrones-dragonstone-script-annotated):
  the Citadel quotation is spoken by Archmaester Ebrose.

Braavos, Free Folk, Faceless Men, Dothraki and Whisper Network use original
engineering mottos, labeled as such in each guide. Do not present those mottos
as quotations from the books or television series. Literary names and quoted
lines remain attributable to their original creators; the MIT license covers
this project's original engineering text and implementation. This project is
not affiliated with the fictional franchise's creators or rights holders.

## Engineering research behind the taxonomy

The final assignments are the author's synthesis, informed by:

- [SWEBOK and SFIA](https://sfia-online.org/en/tools-and-resources/bodies-of-knowledge/swebok-software-engineering-body-of-knowledge/swebok4-sfia9-the-guide-to-the-software-engineering-body-of-knowledge)
- [Agile principles](https://agilemanifesto.org/principles.html)
- [Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html)
- [Kanban Guide](https://kanbanguides.org/the-kanban-guide/)
- [Test-driven development](https://agilealliance.org/glossary/tdd/)
- [Google SRE](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/)
- [NIST secure software development framework](https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/ssdf)

Use the repository's current engineering standards and installed tools when
applying the charter. These references provide background, not an instruction
to impose every framework or control on every task.
